# Level 3: the Tropical Ocean

Level 3 used to be the Ash Volcano (Volcán Ceniza). It is now **the Tropical Ocean (Océano
Tropical)**: a harbour town on the coast of a jungle mainland, open sea, reefs, wrecks and tide
flats, and islands. Four of the islands belong to the guardian spirits, the Tapus. Very rarely,
far out in the fog, there is a fifth: Birth Island.

Levels 1 and 2 are unchanged. Turning the `tropicalOcean` setting off brings the Ash Volcano back
as Level 3.

## The world

Every run starts on the **harbour pier**. The harbour is a painting (`assets/ocean/harbor.webp`)
placed so that the middle of the pier is where you stand. Around you: three boats moored at the
pier, a ferry, crates and barrels, Wingull, palms along the beach, the town, and the
**lighthouse** on the rocky headland to the west.

The mainland carries on from the painting's edges as jungle no one can walk through, with beaches
along its coast. The sea opens to the south and east.

Every world is laid out from its seed, so the same seed always gives the same sea:

| Place | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Thundercrest Island** (Tapu Koko) | black volcanic rock and sand, red crystal rocks, glowing yellow shards; a storm that never leaves; a cliff-top arena with one ramp up |
| **Blossomveil Island** (Tapu Lele) | pale meadows, pink blossom trees, rafflesia and wildflowers, standing stones, petals on the wind |
| **Verdant Crown Island** (Tapu Bulu) | dense jungle of palms and giant trees, ferns, vines, tiki posts, ruined pillars and a ruined plaza |
| **Isle of Lost Souls** (Tapu Fini) | ash and dead grass, dead trees, graves, broken walls, glowing fungi; permanent fog that thickens toward the Summit of Two Orbs |
| **Gull Rock** | a rocky islet with Wingull nests |
| **Slowpoke Cay** | a sandy cay with palms and driftwood in a warm lagoon |
| **Seel Rocks** | the Seel colony's rocks, with a grotto at their foot |
| **Tide-Pool Flats** | rock flats full of little pools (Shellder, Krabby, Staryu) |
| **Whirl Islet** | old ruins over the trench; Lugia's shrine lies just off its shore |
| **The whirlpool** | between two crags, on the way to Whirl Islet |
| **Reefs** | three reef flats: shallow turquoise over coral, with coral heads that stop boats |
| **The sandbar** | off the harbour's beach, joined to it by a tidal causeway |
| **Wrecks** | an old trader on the open sea, and a ghost ship in Fini's fog |
| **The trench** | a band of abyssal water across the south |
| **Birth Island** | only in some worlds (see below) |

### Depth

One raster (16 px cells) covers the whole sea. A distance transform turns it into "how far from
the coast", and with a little noise that gives four classes of water:

| Class | Looks like | Who can be there |
|---|---|---|
| **Shallow** | turquoise over sand, coral showing through on the reefs | everyone (wading slows you down) |
| **Medium** | deep blue | swimmers, fliers, boats |
| **Deep** | navy | swimmers, fliers, boats |
| **Abyssal** | near black: the trench and the far ocean | swimmers, fliers, boats |

Lagoons have wide, warm shallows; reefs raise the shallows far out at sea. The painted islands
have exact 8 px collision grids (water / walkable / solid) made from the art, so buildings,
cliffs, trees and pier posts are solid.

### Tides

Two tides a day (the game's clock): high at 3:00 and 15:00, low at 9:00 and 21:00. The clock
shows the tide while you're at sea.

- The shallows widen at low tide and narrow at high tide.
- **Low tide** bares the sandbar, the causeway out to it, the grotto's floor at Seel Rocks, and
  the old stone path and floor of Lugia's shrine.
- **High tide** drowns them all. Paths that were dry become deep water.
- Shore-dwellers such as Shellder rest wherever the tide has left the water's edge.
- **No one is ever trapped.** If the tide comes in while you're on a tidal path, you can always
  wade back out.

### Currents, the whirlpool, the weather

- Two currents run across the sea, and a slow drift runs everywhere. You can see them as moving
  streaks on the water. They carry boats, and Tentacool ride them.
- The whirlpool turns round and round and pulls inward, spinning boats as they pass. A boat can
  always pull free.
- The weather over the sea is windier and stormier than over the forest. Fog banks surround
  Fini's isle, the ghost wreck and Birth Island, and Thundercrest has its own storm. They blend
  into the sky as you sail in and out of them.

## Boats

- **Boarding:** walk into a boat to climb aboard. From the pier, push toward a boat moored just
  beyond the railing. The usual controls steer it.
- **Handling:**
  - It speeds up and slows down, turns wider when it's going fast, and slides a little
    sideways.
  - It floats and bobs, and leaves a wake.
  - At low speed it can ease away from a pier it can't yet turn from.
- **The sea pushes back, but you always keep control:**
  - Currents carry the boat, and the wind leans on it.
  - Gusts make it skid sideways.
  - Rough water lowers its top speed, and storm waves shove it about.
- **Collisions:** it runs aground on coasts, rocks, coral heads and wrecks, sliding along them
  where it can. It never passes through anything, and it never turns its hull into the pier.
- **Stepping off:** run up against a beach, the pier, an island or the shallows right at the
  shore, and keep pushing toward it.
  - You step onto land, never into open water.
  - The boat stays exactly where you left it, so a way back is always there.
- **Companions:** ones who can't swim (Charmander, Sprigatito…) ride with you. Froakie swims
  alongside.
- **Co-op:** each player's game sails its own boats. A player seen out on deep water is drawn
  in a boat.

## Who moves how

How a species moves decides where it can go, not just its type:

| Moves | Examples | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Swims** (water-bound) | Magikarp, Goldeen, Seaking, Carvanha, Sharpedo, Tentacool, Horsea, Corsola, Chinchou, Lanturn, Wailmer, Staryu, Dhelmise… | only in water, any depth |
| **Shore-dwelling** | Shellder, Slowpoke, Krabby, Corphish, Wimpod, Dewpider, Binacle… | land and shallows, never deeper |
| **Amphibious** | Seel, Dewgong, Psyduck, Poliwag, Marill… | anywhere wet or dry |
| **Flies or floats** | Wingull, Pelipper, Gastly, Drifloon… | over everything |
| **Walks** | everyone else | land and shallows |

So a land Pokémon can never path from one island to another. Your own Pokémon follows the same
rules: **Water types swim** anywhere, and everyone else wades the shallows and takes a boat
beyond.

## Who lives where

Every spawn goes to its kind of place near where the game put it, or doesn't appear. The
spawn bag also leans toward who lives around you:

| Habitat | Pokémon |
|---|---|

| **Reefs** | Goldeen, Seaking, Corsola, Horsea, Seadra, Luvdisc, Qwilfish, Remoraid, Clamperl, Skrelp, Starmie |
| **Currents** | Tentacool, Tentacruel, Mantyke, Mantine |
| **Deep water** | Carvanha, Sharpedo, Chinchou, Lanturn, Wailmer, Wailord, Relicanth, Huntail, Gorebyss, Finneon, Lumineon, Gyarados, Kingdra, Lapras |
| **Wrecks** | Dhelmise; in the ghost wreck's fog, ghosts drift over the water |
| **Tide pools** | Shellder, Staryu, Dewpider, Binacle, Omanyte, Kabuto |
| **Beaches** | Slowpoke (Slowpoke Cay most of all), Krabby, Corphish, Psyduck, Wimpod, Crabrawler |
| **Rocks** | Seel and Dewgong (Seel Rocks) |
| **The air** | Wingull and Pelipper (Gull Rock most of all) |
| **Thundercrest** | Electrike, Shinx, Voltorb, Magnemite, Dedenne, Togedemaru, Charjabug, Elekid, Pikachu… |
| **Blossomveil** | Ralts and its line, Cutiefly, Ribombee, Comfey, Cottonee, Whimsicott |

The big ones are rare (Gyarados, Kingdra, Lapras, Wailord, Milotic, Relicanth). Chinchou,
Lanturn, Finneon and Lumineon come out at night, and Chinchou and Lanturn light up the night
water. Wingull keep to the day.

## The weather and the sea's Pokémon

- **Storms:**
  - Carvanha and Sharpedo turn vicious.
  - Magikarp thrash and leap.
  - Wingull and Pelipper make for land.
  - Seel crowd into their grotto.
  - Tentacool drift in close to shore.
  - Slowpoke doesn't notice anything at all.
- **The water itself:**
  - A storm turns the sea dark navy, almost black, with bigger, whiter waves, spray and
    whitecaps.
  - Rain rings the surface.
  - Sun glints on calm water.
  - At night the breaking waves glow faintly (plankton).

## The guardians

Each spirit waits at the heart of its island. Step into its arena and it comes for you:

- **Tapu Koko** drops in with a thunderbolt on its cliff-top. It is fast and furious, and
  lightning keeps striking around you.
- **Tapu Lele** appears from a swirl of petals in the ring of standing stones. It giggles,
  vanishes and reappears around you, and its psychic rings hurt.
- **Tapu Bulu** rises from the ruined plaza, slow and immensely strong. The ground erupts where
  you stand.
- **Tapu Fini** rises from the fog on the **Summit of Two Orbs** when you reach for the Red Orb
  and the Blue Orb on their pedestals. Rings of water gather in the mist.
  - It tests you **once and only once**: win, and **both orbs are yours to keep**.
  - Fini itself stays as the isle's guardian.
  - Kyogre and Groudon are not obtainable.

Beat Koko, Lele or Bulu and it joins you (choose it as your partner from the menu), like the
Shady Forest's legendary beasts. Lose or leave, and it goes back to wait for you.

## Birth Island and Deoxys

- **Where:** in some worlds (35%, plus 20% for every world that didn't have it, so there's no
  grinding), Birth Island lies far out to sea inside its own fog bank.
- **Its look:** as on the Game Boy Advance (`assets/ocean/birth.png`, drawn at twice its
  pixels): the stepped triangle of rock, red cliffs, the pier and the little house.
- **Finding it:** whenever you're anywhere near, its signal pulses red at the edge of the
  screen.
- **The puzzle:**
  - On the bare rock at the top sits the black triangle. Touch it and it jumps somewhere else.
  - Reach it again before you've taken too many steps, or it slips back to the middle. The count
    starts over at each touch.
  - Its red glow burns brighter with every touch.
- **Deoxys:** touch the triangle at the last place and it splits open. Win, and Deoxys is
  yours, kept with your save. Lose, and the triangle waits at the centre again.

## Manaphy, Phione and the feather

- **When:** rarely, in calm, clear daytime water, a few Phione (or Manaphy) play at the surface.
- **They can't be caught.** Come close and they dive and are gone.
- **The feather:** Manaphy sometimes leaves something floating behind, the **Strong Silver
  Feather**. You can pick it up from a boat. It's kept between runs.

## Lugia's shrine

- **Where:** a shrine lies sunk off Whirl Islet, its floor marked with wings over waves and
  ringed by old pillars.
- **Getting there:**
  - At low tide the sea draws back from an old stone path and the shrine's floor, so anyone can
    walk there.
  - Water types can swim there at any time.
- **With the feather:** stand at the shrine's heart carrying the Strong Silver Feather, and its
  heart glows. Something enormous passes overhead and speaks to you in the Legendary box. If
  you also hold both orbs, it warns you about them.
- **Lugia is not obtainable.**

## Kyogre

Kyogre is only ever spoken of. Inscriptions tell of "one who raised the seas" sleeping beneath
the trench. At night, very deep in the trench, lines of light glow red and blue, more brightly if
you carry the Blue Orb. It is never encountered and never obtainable.

## Inscriptions

Eight stones tell the sea's history, in any order: three at the shrine, two on Whirl Islet, and
one each on the Isle of Lost Souls, Verdant Crown and Blossomveil. They cover the war of sea and
land, the guardian of the storm, the two orbs and the island spirits who keep them, the feather,
and the sleeper in the trench. Stand by one to read it. Unread stones glow faintly.

## Now and then

- **A great shadow:** an enormous shadow passes under your boat in deep water. Sometimes a
  Wailord surfaces far off to blow; sometimes nothing ever does.
- **Leaping Magikarp:** a school of them jumps out of the water near you.
- **Fish schools:** small fish move through the clear shallows and scatter from you.
- **At night:** the shrine and the unread stones glow.
- **Discoveries:** each island's name appears the first time you set foot on it. The party
  reacts to the boats, islands, wrecks, the ghost ship, the storms, the fog, the lighthouse and
  the great shadow.

## The lighthouse

- **The beam:** at night, in fog, rain or a storm, the lighthouse sweeps two beams across the
  sea, and its light shows in the darkness.
- **The way home:** when you can't see it but its light would carry, a glow at the edge of the
  screen (brighter as the beam swings your way) shows which way home is.

## No softlocks

- Walking off land into deep water just stops you at the edge.
- Anything dropped where it can't be is moved to the nearest place it can (or, at sea, quietly
  leaves).
- You never step off a boat into open water, and a boat stays wherever you left it.
- On a tidal path when the tide comes in, you can always wade out.
- A boat can always pull free of the whirlpool, and it never wedges its hull into the pier.
- Every guardian, Deoxys and Lugia's visit can be retried if you lose or leave.

## Performance

- **The raster:** built once per world (about 150 ms).
- **The sea's chunks:** painted once and kept (about 13 ms each, the forest's cost or less).
  As the tide turns they repaint one per frame.
- **Each frame:** the water's animation is a few half-resolution overlay passes, about 2 ms.
- **Depth lookups:** a sample of the sea costs about 1.4 µs.

## Settings

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `tropicalOcean` | on | Level 3 is the Tropical Ocean (off: the Ash Volcano) |
| `birthIslandChance` | 0.35 | the share of worlds with Birth Island (plus 0.2 for each world without it) |
| `oceanTide` | null | null: tides follow the clock; −1 (low) … 1 (high) holds the tide there |
| `boats`, `seaStories`, `guardians`, `birthIsland` | on | each part can be switched off |
| `manaphyChance` | 0.12 | how likely Manaphy or Phione are to appear, at each chance |

### Console helpers

```js
PSXAI.Ocean.plan()               // the islands, reefs, wrecks, currents, tidal paths…
PSXAI.Ocean.sample(x, y)         // the sea at a point: depth class, tide, reef, solid
PSXAI.Boats.boats                // the boats
PSXAI.Sea.keyItems()             // the key items you have
PSXAI.Guardians.startScene('koko')
PSXAI.Birth.isle()               // Birth Island, if this world has it
PSXAI.Sea.forget()               // forget everything the sea saved
```

## What's not here

- **Frillish and Jellicent** aren't in the game's Pokédex at all, so the ghost wreck has Dhelmise
  and the ghosts that drift over the water.
- **Lugia's shrine:** it is a sunken shrine the low tide bares, not a separate underwater
  interior.
- **Boats in co-op** are local to each player's game.
- **The supplied island map** (the guardian island picture) isn't used as a whole island. It's
  a small, watermarked JPEG, so its palms, blossom trees, rocks, shrine, tiki posts, rafflesia
  and hut were cut out as props instead. The tropical route map isn't used.
- **Portraits:** the Tapus', Deoxys's, Lugia's, Manaphy's and Phione's dialogue portraits ship
  with the game (`assets/portraits`, from SpriteCollab). Tapu Bulu and Tapu Fini only have a
  Normal expression there, so they keep it whatever they say.
