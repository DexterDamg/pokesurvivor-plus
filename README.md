# PokéSurvivor+

A modded version of [PokéSurvivor](https://github.com/JPTER78/pokesurvivor) by JPTER78: a free, non-profit Pokémon survival fan game that runs in your browser.

**Play:** https://intox2b20.github.io/pokesurvivor-plus/

By default this public version runs without any server: your progress is saved in your browser (no online accounts, friends, co-op or leaderboard).

**Want to play with your friends?** Make your own copy and give it **your own free server** (accounts, friends, co-op, leaderboard) on Firebase: see **[SERVER_SETUP.md](SERVER_SETUP.md)** (beginner-friendly, about 30-45 minutes, no coding).

New in this version: power crystals and random events (Jigglypuff, egg, Jirachi), golden rifts, fixed rift/boss schedule, Z-Moves, TMs, new items, League of Legends–style HUD, targeting options, Traditional Chinese, and more. See "What's new" in the game.

**Living World AI:** wild Pokémon now have temperaments, emotions (shown as little bubbles), daily routines, friendships, grudges and their own fights, react to Legendaries, and can evolve on the spot. Not everything is hostile anymore. Press **1–4** to stop or resume a move's automatic attacks, click or tap a Pokémon to mark it as your target, and press **F7** for settings. Details: **[docs/LIVING_WORLD_AI.md](docs/LIVING_WORLD_AI.md)**.

**Living Environment:** a day/night cycle with real lighting (sunlight and shadows, moonlight, lanterns), living weather (rain that leaves puddles and mud, fog from faint mist to a white wall, gales, thunderstorms), new trees and forest decor, an ambient soundscape, and areas with their own Pokémon. The starting woodland is home to Pidgey, Rattata, Caterpie, Hoothoot and friends, and some of them whisper about an old mansion hidden in the woods. Press **L** for your lantern. Forests are open and natural, with trees that grow from saplings to rare ancient giants; every Pokémon is sized by its species; storms are dark and dramatic (with a setting to tone down the flashes); and bosses and rifts come as you defeat Pokémon instead of on a timer. Details: **[docs/LIVING_ENVIRONMENT.md](docs/LIVING_ENVIRONMENT.md)**.

**The Haunted Mansion:** a lantern-lit dirt path leads from the Shady Forest to the gates of an old mansion. Inside: knocking behind locked doors, footsteps in empty halls, portraits that change, and a tall mirror at the end of the hall. A group of Ghost Pokémon has some explaining to do, in proper Mystery Dungeon-style dialogue. And something else was watching. Details: **[docs/HAUNTED_MANSION.md](docs/HAUNTED_MANSION.md)**.

**The Shady Forest, rebuilt:** the forest floor is one continuous, darker woodland surface (no tile grid, no sparkles), laid out as regions that blend into each other: outer woodland, ancient groves, wetwood lowlands, the deep forest and sunlit clearings. A great lake with muddy shores, shallows and deep water reflects the trees and ripples in the rain; deep water stops you and every Pokémon that can't swim, fly or float. Logs, stones and stumps are solid. Wild Pokémon no longer show health bars, and their evolutions and arrivals happen quietly. And the forest keeps its secrets: rumours, strange signs and rare glimpses of things that shouldn't exist, written down without a single name in a one-page journal (**J**; the game pauses while you read). Details: **[docs/SHADY_FOREST.md](docs/SHADY_FOREST.md)**.

**Natures and dialogue:** all 25 **Natures** now shape how every Pokémon behaves, not just its stats. Pokémon that matter speak in Mystery Dungeon dialogue boxes with PMD portraits (you and your friends on the left, important characters on the right, and a gold box that only Legendary Pokémon may use). Details: **[docs/NATURES_AND_DIALOGUE.md](docs/NATURES_AND_DIALOGUE.md)**.

**Darkness, light and a living forest:** nights are truly dark, lit only by faint moonlight (its phase shows in the water), lanterns with oil, glowing Pokémon (Charmander's tail, Ampharos's orb, Litwick's flame), and moves that light up the night for a moment. Wildlife lives by its biology: cocoons cling to trees, caterpillars climb them, birds perch and take off. The forest has events (a lost Pokémon, strange lights, a sudden silence, an abandoned camp) and a history to piece together; exploring, treasure and kindness give EXP too. After 20 minutes, the old mansion appears ahead of you as the Shady Forest's end stage, and the woods start pointing the way. Weather moves on about once a minute, smoothly; at night only the night Pokémon are about. Some runs hide an old, creepy well. And the legendary beasts can be won: four shadows of **Raikou** in a harsh night storm, four of **Suicune** in the thickest daytime fog, and then **Entei** on a clear day. Details: **[docs/SHADY_FOREST.md](docs/SHADY_FOREST.md)** and **[docs/LIVING_ENVIRONMENT.md](docs/LIVING_ENVIRONMENT.md)**.

**Level 3 is now the Tropical Ocean:** you start on the pier of a harbour town with boats moored beside it and a lighthouse on the headland. Beyond it lies an ocean of islands, reefs, wrecks and tide flats. The water has four depths (turquoise shallows anyone can wade, deep blue, navy and the near-black trench). Walk into a boat to sail it: it has weight, the currents and wind push it, storms shove it about, and you step off onto a beach or the pier wherever you like. Fish never leave the water, shore-dwellers keep to the coast, Water-type players swim, and nobody walks from one island to another. Tides bare sandbars and paths and drown them again. Four guardian islands each have their Tapu waiting at the heart; Tapu Fini's challenge on the fog-bound Isle of Lost Souls gives you the Red and Blue Orbs to keep. If you're lucky, Birth Island waits far out in the fog with its triangle puzzle and Deoxys. Manaphy and Phione play at the surface (and flee), Manaphy may leave a feather, and with it Lugia's sunken shrine wakes. Kyogre is only ever spoken of. Details: **[docs/TROPICAL_OCEAN.md](docs/TROPICAL_OCEAN.md)**.

**Mega Evolution and Z-Moves are earned:** Mega Evolution unlocks by beating the Crystal Cave and needs your Pokémon's own Mega Stone, a held item (1% on level-up, 5% from chests, 10% from big chests, or one silent glowing stone somewhere in the world). Z-Moves unlock by beating Level 3, the Tropical Ocean, and their slot doesn't even appear until you've won the right Z-Crystal in a themed Totem Trial maze. Details: **[docs/POWER_PROGRESSION.md](docs/POWER_PROGRESSION.md)**.
